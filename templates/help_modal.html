How to Use This Homepage

Editing Bookmarks: To add, edit, or remove bookmarks, click the "Edit Bookmark" button in the user menu. This will enter edit mode, allowing you to manage your bookmarks.

Customizing Themes: You can change the look and feel of your homepage by clicking "Edit Theme" in the user menu. Choose from different colors and patterns to personalize your experience.

Syncing Across Devices: By creating an account, your bookmarks and settings will be automatically synced across all your devices. Simply log in to access your personalized homepage from anywhere.

Setting as Homepage: To set this page as your browser's homepage, please do so manually in your browser's settings. For security reasons, websites cannot do this automatically.
